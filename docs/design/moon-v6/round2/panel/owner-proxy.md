# Owner's proxy verdict: round 2 moons and icon

Juror: owner-proxy. I judged from rendered PNGs only and did not open any SVG source. I recorded every blind association below before I opened `blind_key.txt`.

The owner's bar, restated: round 1 was rejected as "not a fan of the icons", with a reflection that "doesn't match the moon", and a request for "more detail, more FFXIV inspiration". The moons must not look like cheese. They should be calm and static with flair and character, carry no meaningless decorative glyphs, and stay safety-minded.

## Stock-icon failures

These are the states that failed to read as a moon, or read as a stock UI icon, at row size (16 px). Hero size is noted where it differs.

- **A:**
  - Locked out reads as a no-entry sign at both sizes.
  - Done reads as a check-circle.
  - Completed reads as a coin at both sizes.
  - Not checked reads as a hamburger at 16 px.
  - In journal reads as a contrast toggle at 16 px.
  - 3 of 8 fail outright.
- **B:**
  - Ready reads as a light bulb or user icon at 16 px. It is the most important state, and it is also the smallest moon.
  - Locked out reads as a no-entry sign at 16 px.
  - Not checked reads as a hamburger at 16 px and as Saturn at 48 px.
  - Ready on another job reads as a contrast toggle at 16 px.
  - Completed reads as a coin.
  - At 48 px, the 24 rim ticks make several states look like **suns with rays**.
  - 4 of 8 fail at row size.
- **C:**
  - Locked out reads as a no-entry sign at both sizes.
  - Completed reads as a plain dot or coin.
  - Done reads as a check-circle.
  - Not checked reads as a crosshair and is nearly invisible.
  - Ready reads as a lollipop or hand mirror.
  - Ready on another job reads as an AI sparkle at 16 px.
  - 5 of 8 are weak or fail.
- **D:**
  - Completed reads as a coin or radio button.
  - Done reads as a check-circle.
  - Blocked reads as a radio button at 16 px.
  - Not checked reads as a hamburger in a ring at 16 px.
  - Ready on another job reads as a contrast toggle.
  - The rim on every glyph turns the whole set into "buttons" at row size.
  - Locked out is the only red state in the field that does **not** read as a no-entry sign.
  - At 48 px all 8 read as moon phenomena.

No concept reads as cheese, which is a real win over round 1. No concept uses an AI four-point star, apart from C's sparkle at 16 px.

## Best glyph set: D (menphina-medallion)

D is the only set where every glyph reads as a moon phenomenon at 48 px. It is the only one whose Locked out avoids the no-entry sign, which matters for a safety-minded owner. Its job-icon medallion build answers "more FFXIV inspiration" the most directly, and Ready is literally the plugin icon in miniature.

A is a close second. It is cleaner at row size and has the best distinctness numbers, but it fails three stock reads at row size (no-entry, coin, check-circle).

## Best icon: D (menphina-medallion)

D is the only icon that answers both halves of the owner's rejection:

- The reflection is visibly the crescent's own light, directly under it, in the same colour, broken by ripples and lens-shaped.
- It has more detail and FFXIV flavour: the lantern, the aetheryte island and the gilt filigree.

C has the richest single motif in the Ishgard skyline, but its framed moon breaks the reflection logic the owner called out by name.

## What must change before the owner sees it

**D (to be shown):**
- **Thin the rims at the row tier.** At 16–20 px, drop or halve the medallion rim on the non-act states so Blocked, Completed and Ready on another job stop reading as radio buttons and contrast toggles. Keep the full rim from 24 px up.
- **Done this cycle.** The check in a ring is a check-circle. Make the check smaller and tuck it into the dark limb, or use the waning-gibbous shape alone with a small gilt tick outside the disc.
- **Completed.** At 16 px it is a flat coin. Add a visible pale limb band or a soft lit edge so it reads as a full moon, not a token.
- **Not checked.** The ring plus two bars reads as a hamburger. Drop the ghost ring and let the wisps break the disc's outline, as A does.
- **Ready on another job.** The crystal must be legible at 16 px. At present it is a half-disc toggle.
- **Icon.** Bring the glint peak to about 85% of the crescent's lightness, and vary the glint shapes so they read as light on water, not leaves. Keep the road out of the Installed-check corner.
- **Tokens.** Fold iron and oxidised pewter into pewter to meet the token budget.

**A:**
- **Locked out.** Replace the diagonal ofuda with D's cracked Dalamud, or with a band that does not cross the disc at 45°.
- **Completed.** Remove the faceted "gem coin" look and the wide bezel.
- **Done.** Give it the same check-circle fix as D.
- **Icon.** Spread the shard tower into a ragged lens of horizontal glints. Add one real FFXIV landmark, because the speck of an aetheryte is not enough.

**B:**
- **Rim ticks.** Delete the 24 rim ticks below 96 px. They are sun rays and banned noise.
- **Ready.** Rebuild Ready at full size. It is a light bulb at 16 px.
- **Locked out.** The vertical strip reads as a glitch. Rework it.
- **Not checked.** It reads as Saturn. Rework it.
- **Icon.** Replace the pill bricks with irregular glints. Match the moon's hue to the road. Make the ticked ring read as an astrolabe and not a clock, or drop it.

**C:**
- **Not checked.** Raise its salience. At 15 it vanishes on the daylight overlay.
- **Locked out, Completed and Ready.** Fix the no-entry slash, the plain dot and the lollipop.
- **Icon.** Remove the gilt frame from the sky moon, because a framed plate cannot cast that reflection. Replace the hex paving pyramid with a lens of glints. Keep the Ishgard skyline: it is the best FFXIV detail in the round and worth borrowing.
