**Every miss in every concept is the same swap.** All four put the check mark on *Done this cycle* and give *Completed* a plain full disc. To a new player a check mark means "finished", full stop. A blank full moon means nothing in particular, so I filed it under "done for now". This is a shared design-system problem, not a weakness of one concept. The other six states were guessable in all four. Ready (glow plus road), In journal (bookmark), Ready on another job (crystal = job), Locked out (red), Blocked (dark, barely lit) and Not checked (clouded over) all landed for me.

## Best glyph set: D (menphina-medallion)

It is the set I'd understand fastest on day one. Ready is the plugin icon shrunk down, so I'd learn "gold moon over water = go do it" without a legend. The cracked red moon reads as "broken / never" without borrowing the no-entry sign that A and C use. The medallion rims also make it look like it belongs next to my job icons instead of looking like a web UI.

## Best icon: D

In the installer list it is the one I'd click: a big clean crescent, a lantern glowing on the shore, and a moonlit path on the water toward a crystal island. It also reads as "a road by moonlight" at 32px, and it is the only icon whose shapes survive the Installed check and the greyed Disabled state without losing the moon.

## What must change

**All concepts (blocking):**
- Swap the meaning carriers for Completed and Done this cycle. Either put the check on **Completed**, or give Completed its own unmistakable "finished" mark and give Done this cycle a "comes back" mark (a small circular arrow or a clock-hand notch on the waning half) instead of a check. As drawn, every new player will read the check as "finished forever" and the bare full moon as "nothing here".
- Not checked should not be two horizontal bars across a disc, which reads as a hamburger or menu icon at 16px. Try a single soft veil or a fainter, dashed limb with one wisp.
- Separate Completed, Blocked and Not checked at 16px by value or outline, not hairlines. Right now they are three grey dots in every set.

**A:** Replace the diagonal ofuda on Locked out (it is a no-entry sign at 16px). Use D's fracture idea or a vertical, off-centre seal. Make the aetheryte in the icon big enough to see at 64px.

**B:** Replace Ready's two pills with real glints (they read as UI buttons). Pull the Not checked bands back inside or just past the rim. Widen the Locked out strip or the red mass so it holds on daylight scenes. Drop the 24-tick halos unless they mean something. Rebuild the icon road so it is dimmer than the moon and broken, not a brick stack.

**C:** Replace the diagonal band on Locked out (no-entry). Give Completed at least a visible bezel or inlay at 20px so it isn't a radio-button dot. Make the icon moon read as a moon rather than a gold-framed lemon slice: drop the gold came on the icon or show the crescent shape more strongly. Taper the hexagon road so it is not a pyramid.

**D:** The ring on every state edges toward radio buttons and settings toggles. Try dropping the rim to a thinner hairline on the pewter states at row size, so Blocked stops looking like a contrast toggle. Make Completed visibly "finished" at row size (see the swap above). Keep the fracture, the Ready sea and the icon as they are.
