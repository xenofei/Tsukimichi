## Stock-icon failures

These are the glyphs that didn't read as a moon phenomenon in the blind pass, or that read first as a stock UI icon.

- **A:** Locked out reads as a no-entry sign. Done reads as a check-circle. Not checked reads as a hamburger at 16 px. Completed reads as a coin or marble. Ready reads as a lollipop or bulb at 16 px, but it is unmistakable, which is good.
- **B:** Completed reads as a coin, the worst of the four. Ready on another job reads as a contrast toggle. Done reads as a check-circle. Not checked reads as Saturn or a hamburger. Ready reads as a light bulb or user pin. Locked out is a vertical slash that reads as a cut fruit, neither a moon nor clearly "locked". The tick-mark aura on several states reads as a spinner or sun rays.
- **C:** Locked out reads as a no-entry sign. Completed reads as a radio button or plain dot. Done reads as a check-circle. In journal reads as a contrast toggle. Ready on another job's diagonal sheen plus blue sparkle reads as an AI sparkle, and at 16 px it shares Locked out's diagonal-split silhouette. Not checked nearly vanishes.
- **D:** Completed and Blocked together read as a **filled vs. hollow radio button** in rows: an outlined ring next to a filled disc, both in the same medallion. Ready on another job and In journal both read as contrast toggles inside a ring. Done reads as a check-circle. Not checked reads as minus-in-circle. Locked out avoids the no-entry trap. Blocked (eclipse) is the best Blocked of the four.

All four use a check for Done. I accept a check as functional, but it is the one stock icon every concept kept.

## Best glyph set + why

**A (aether-crystal).** It is the only set where Ready grabs me instantly in every row, over both scenes. It has the best measured and visual distinctness. Blocked, Completed and Not checked don't collapse into the same grey dot as badly as they do in B and C. D is the prettier, more FFXIV family, but its uniform medallion ring makes Ready no louder than its neighbours. That fails the one job I need the icons to do.

## Best icon + why

**D (menphina-medallion).** It has the most Eorzean scene (lantern, aetheryte islet), the road is physically honest, the crescent stays clean at 32 px, and it is calm. A is a close second and fits its own glyph set more tightly. If A's glyphs win, take D's lantern and crystal-islet staging into A's icon, or give D's icon A's crystal shards.

## What must change

- **A:** Replace Locked out's diagonal ribbon with something that is not a slash-in-circle (D's crack, or a chained or eclipsed blood moon). Give Completed a clearer full-moon read (a limb highlight, or a gilt or pewter rim distinct from Blocked) so it stops being a coin. Make Not checked a hazy moon with visible limb, not three bars. Keep Ready's road and stop relying on the glow for salience.
- **B:** Remove the tick aura at every size below 48 px. Completed must lose the gold ring (it reads as a coin). Replace the two pills under Ready with real glints. Use a different mark on Locked out than the vertical ribbon. Separate Ready on another job from In journal: same half disc plus a vertical mark is too close. Rebuild the icon road so it isn't bricks.
- **C:** Make Not checked visible over daylight. Give Completed a rim or limb so it isn't a dot, and change the Locked out slash. Ready on another job's diagonal sheen must not echo Locked out. Cool the ivory lit glass toward moonstone silver. Break up the icon's hex pyramid and dim it below the moon.
- **D:** Make Ready break the circle at row size: let the road glints hang below the bezel (as A, B and C do), or brighten the moon and gild the whole bezel heavier. Split the Blocked/Completed radio-button pair by giving Completed a visible full-moon face or limb light. Give Ready on another job a non-half-disc phase (a crescent, as A does) so it isn't a contrast toggle and stops matching In journal and Blocked. Keep the crack on Locked out.
