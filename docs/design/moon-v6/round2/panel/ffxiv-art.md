## Best glyph set and why

**D, menphina-medallion.** It is the only set that speaks FFXIV's icon grammar at every size: a bevelled pewter or gilt rim, a dark enamel well, and a light emblem, as on job and role icons. Gilt appears only on the state that wants action. It is also the only set that solves Locked out with lore instead of a prohibition sign: a fractured red moon is unmistakably Dalamud and reads at 16 px. Ready as a tiny moon-over-sea medal makes the icon and the glyphs one family. A is a close second and has better distinctness numbers, but its sash-style Locked out and teal Ready glow cost it.

## Best icon and why

**D.** It has the most physically honest reflection: the column sits under the lit crescent, matches its hue, stays below its brightness, and breaks into ripples. The small lantern reflection proves the rule rather than breaking it. At 32 px and under the disabled and installed overlays it still reads as moon, road and lantern. A is a good second. B's brick road and C's tesserae pyramid both fail the "not a pyramid" test.

## What must change

**A: aether-crystal**
- Locked out: drop the diagonal sash, which reads as a no-entry sign. Use a vertical ofuda tied over a red moon, or the fracture idea.
- Ready: remove the teal glow at 16 to 20 px. Let the gilt rim and glints carry salience.
- Completed: keep the gilt engraved bezel visible from 20 px up (a 1.5 px gilt ring) so it stops reading as a coin.
- Not checked: keep the wisps inside the disc and raise salience above 20.
- Icon: lengthen and flatten the glints into horizontal ripple streaks, so they read as light on water rather than crystal stepping stones.

**B: astrologian-orrery**
- Remove the 24-tick ring from every glyph. At most, keep four cardinal notches on Ready only, because the ticks plus glow make a sun.
- Ready: replace the two pills under the moon (which read as a user icon) with broken lozenge glints.
- Locked out: the near-vertical strip reads as a slash or bracket. Redesign it as a sealed ofuda across a full red disc, or a fracture.
- Not checked: keep the mist bars inside the outline.
- Completed: add an inner mark so the gilt-rimmed disc is not a coin.
- Icon: replace the brick rectangles with ragged elongated glints. Dim them to about 90% of the moon. Make the moon a crescent or a thinner gibbous so it stops reading as an egg.

**C: ishgard-glass**
- Change the lit glass from ivory to cool moonstone, and use a curved terminator instead of the straight diagonal cut. That fixes the cheese, lemon slice and lollipop reads.
- Thicken the came to about 1 px at 20 px on two or three lines, so the concept survives in-game rather than only at 96 px.
- Locked out: remove the diagonal sash, which reads as a no-entry sign.
- Not checked: raise salience from 15. It currently vanishes on the daylight overlay.
- Ready on another job: move the crystal off the rim, where it reads as an AI sparkle.
- Icon: reshape the hex tesserae into horizontal ripple streaks narrowing toward the horizon, not a triangle. Slim the gilt came on the moon so it is not a clock face.

**D: menphina-medallion**
- Done this cycle: break the check-circle read. Let the check overlap the rim or sit off-centre as a gilt stamp, and keep the half-lit moon dominant.
- Ready on another job: enlarge the crystal or set it off-axis, so the half disc in a ring stops reading as a contrast toggle. This pair (12.3) is the weakest.
- Not checked: replace the straight bars with curved mist arcs clipped to the enamel, to lose the hamburger read.
- Completed: give the full moon a faint gilt inner ring or let the moon-daisy survive at 20 px, so it is not a disabled radio button.
- Rims: make the rim on resting states slightly thinner or darker, so the column reads as a calm medal row rather than radio buttons.
- Icon: nudge the road and crescent slightly left, clear of the installed check (the designer flags this too). Give the crescent one quiet facet or earthshine hint, so the moon carries as much detail as the frame and lantern.
