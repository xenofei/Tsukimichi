## Scores

### Glyphs

| | Identity 15% | FFXIV 15% | Craft 15% | Legibility 15% | Distinct 10% | Meaning 10% | Coherence 10% | Taste 10% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A aether-crystal | 6 | 6 | 6 | 7 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 6 |
| B astrologian-orrery | 5 | 7 | 5 | 6 | 6 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| C ishgard-glass | 5 | 6 | 6 | 5 | 6 | 5 | 6 | 6 |
| D menphina-medallion | 7 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 7 |

**A, aether-crystal**
- **Identity 6.** Seven of eight read as moons at 48, but Locked out is a textbook no-entry sign and Completed's kite facet reads as a gem or coin.
- **FFXIV 6.** Faceted moonstone and the job crystal feel Eorzean, but this is a generic gem treatment more than anything you'd see in-game.
- **Craft 6.** The hairline facets are lovely at 96. Ready's teal aether halo, though, is the one cheap note in the set: it looks like a mobile-game "power-up available" glow, and the pebble glints under it are lumpy.
- **Legibility 7.** Holds well at 100% and 150%. Not checked fades to nearly nothing on the daylight overlay.
- **Distinct 7.** Silhouettes vary well: crescent, half, gibbous, wisps and band.
- **Meaning 6.** Bookmark, crystal and new moon explain themselves. The band and check-circle borrow stock meaning instead of moon meaning.
- **Coherence 7.** One moonstone palette, and the gilt-means-act / pewter-means-wait grammar is followed strictly.
- **Taste 6.** Calm overall, but the halo breaks the calm.

**B, astrologian-orrery**
- **Identity 5.** The tick ring makes Completed a sun or coin, Ready a lightbulb on two pills, and Not checked Saturn.
- **FFXIV 7.** The most lore in the set: constellation engraving on Blocked (gorgeous), an astrolabe scale, Dalamud, an ember ribbon and a gold check.
- **Craft 5.** The white-and-gold pill bars under Ready look like a toggle switch, which is cheap. The 24 ticks blur into fuzzy rays at 28–48. Locked out's vertical strip on a half-shaded red disc looks like a torn wrapper.
- **Legibility 6.** Ready is bright, but Ready on another job, In journal and Blocked are close at 16 px.
- **Distinct 6.** Three dark-blue half-ish moons cluster together.
- **Meaning 5.** The gold coin "Completed" competes with Ready for "important".
- **Coherence 5.** Gold metal appears on both Ready and Completed, which muddies the grammar.
- **Taste 5.** The tick rays are exactly the "meaningless decoration" the owner rejects.

**C, ishgard-glass**
- **Identity 5.** Completed is a plain radio button and Locked out is a no-entry sign. The glass is only visible at 96 px.
- **FFXIV 6.** Ishgard glass is the best idea on paper, but the came lines vanish below 48 px, so the glyphs read as generic. Only the icon says Ishgard.
- **Craft 6.** The hero glass is genuinely refined. The warm ivory plus thick gilt on Ready clashes with the cool moonstone on every other state.
- **Legibility 5.** Not checked nearly disappears on both overlays, and Completed is a blank dot.
- **Distinct 6.** Weakest pair: Blocked and Not checked.
- **Meaning 5.** Ready on another job's diagonal sheen plus a crystal reads as a sparkle, not "another job".
- **Coherence 6.** There is a shared material, but the tokens sprawl.
- **Taste 6.** The idea is calm, but the payoff is invisible at row size.

**D, menphina-medallion**
- **Identity 7.** Ready (crescent over water), Blocked (eclipse), Locked out (cracked blood moon), Ready on another job and In journal are unmistakably moon events. Completed and Not checked are weak at 16.
- **FFXIV 8.** The whole set is built like job-icon medallions: bevelled metal, enamel well and minted emblem. The Dalamud fracture and Menphina's daisy are real lore choices.
- **Craft 8.** The metal is the most consistent of the four: the same bevel, keyline and pewter on every rim, with gilt only on Ready. Nothing muddy, and the lapis enamel on Ready is beautiful.
- **Legibility 8.** The rims hold the glyphs on the daylight overlay, where A and C wash out. It is the cleanest of the four rows_mock panels.
- **Distinct 7.** Every state has its own emblem. Ready on another job, Blocked and In journal are a little close in tone at 16.
- **Meaning 6.** Done reads as Pac-Man with a check. The crack says "broken or locked" well.
- **Coherence 7.** The tokens are over budget, but visually it is the most unified family, and Ready really is the icon in miniature.
- **Taste 7.** Calm and detailed, and nothing is cheesy. The ring on every glyph is a touch heavy at 16, and Completed's rosette is borderline decoration.

## Best glyph set: D, menphina-medallion

It is the only set I'd call a *set*. Every glyph is minted from the same metal with the same bevel and keyline, like a row of job icons, and gold only ever means "act now". Ready is a gorgeous little lapis-and-gilt moon-over-water medal that I would recognise anywhere. Locked out is the only red moon in the panel that doesn't look like a traffic sign. On the daylight overlay it is the only set whose glyphs don't dissolve. Its weaknesses (Completed and Not checked at 16) are in quiet states where a little invisibility is forgivable.

## Best icon: D, menphina-medallion

It is the one I'd screenshot. A crisp thick crescent, an honest moon road beneath it, a warm lantern answering the cool moon, and a far aetheryte, all at the right restraint. It is the clearest at 64 and 32 in the installer, the strongest under Disabled, and it has no egg, porthole or brick read. C has the better FFXIV setting, but its moon looks like citrus.

## What must change

**A, aether-crystal**
- Remove or sharply reduce Ready's teal halo. Let the gilt bezel and road carry salience, as D does with lit enamel.
- Replace the diagonal ofuda on Locked out, which is still a no-entry sign. Make it off-axis, or adopt a fracture.
- Completed's kite facet reads as a gem or hexagon. Give it a full-moon cue that survives at 16, such as a brighter limb.
- Icon: flatten and soften the crystal glints so they read as light on water, not pebbles. Make the aetheryte larger, or add a second FFXIV landmark that survives at 64.

**B, astrologian-orrery**
- Remove the 24-tick ray ring from the glyphs, or engrave it *inside* the rim. Outside the rim, the ticks read as sun rays.
- Replace Ready's two pill bars with real tapered glints.
- Put Completed in pewter, not gold, so it stops being a coin and stops competing with Ready.
- Stagger and shorten the Not checked bands to kill the Saturn read.
- Icon: swap the bricks for tapered, irregular lozenges at lower brightness, and lighten the notches so the ring stops reading as a clock.

**C, ishgard-glass**
- Locked out is still a no-entry sign. Change the composition.
- Completed needs a rim or came that survives at 16. Right now it is a radio button.
- Raise Not checked's contrast. It vanishes on both overlays.
- Ready on another job: drop the diagonal sheen behind the crystal. Together they read as an AI sparkle.
- Icon moon: cool the ivory toward moonstone, thin the gilt came, and curve the lead lines along the terminator so it stops being a lemon or egg slice.
- Icon road: vary tile sizes and shapes so it stops reading as cobblestones.

**D, menphina-medallion**
- Completed: add a cue that survives at 16 px, such as a lighter limb band or brighter face, so it isn't a disabled radio button. Drop the daisy if it costs clarity.
- Not checked: break the two mist bands' symmetry and let them overlap the rim unevenly, so it is not a hamburger in a ring.
- Done: shape the lit part as a true waning half with a curved terminator. The current notch reads as Pac-Man.
- Icon glints: vary their shapes and lengths more, and drop peak brightness a step so they stop reading as fish scales. Pull the road's right edge out of the Installed-check corner.
- Fold iron and oxidised pewter into the main pewter tokens, as the owner's coherence budget asks.
- At 16 px, slightly thin the rims on non-Ready states so the set is less ring-heavy in a long list.
