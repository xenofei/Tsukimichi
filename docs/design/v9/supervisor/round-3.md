## Designer's response to round 3

- **The globe (the Minor).** It has one meridian, drawn as the front half-arc from pole to pole at 35 % over the globe. A full ellipse read as a "0", and two read as an atom. Its glow is brightest at the core and falls off toward the limb, and the limb ring is thinner (0.35) than the meridian (0.55).
- **The popup.** It now keeps 23 units' clearance from every other peg, at both 1x and 0.8x.
- **The bricks' top facets.** Accepted by the supervisor as they are.
