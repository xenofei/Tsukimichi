## Per level

**6-1 The Armillary (base-26), REVISE (M1, m3, n3).** The lattice idea is good: two crown arcs over the launcher and four spokes give real structure for an opener, the rings carry 60 pegs, and it plays generously (sweep mean 10.1, greedy 29). As a picture it doesn't read as an armillary. A real one is a brass instrument, and the painter drew brass, but the grade turns everything teal and the cage becomes a smudged tangle of ellipses with glow blotches at the spoke ends. That teal-on-violet orb is also exactly what the finale uses (M1). Fix: regrade 6-1 so its second jewel moves well away from 214, ideally toward the painted brass with the cage as pale gold or silver light, or put the sky on a different night (deep blue rather than violet). Keep the pegs and bricks as they are, and re-run F6/protan, since oranges on gold need care. Also give the moon a surface (m3).

**6-2 The Wheel of the Twelve (base-27), REVISE (M2).** This is the most charming board in the stage. The twelve glyphs (Althyk's hourglass, Menphina's crescent, Nald'thal's scales and the rest) read at 640, the magenta nebula band gives the stage its only warm hue, and the observatory is a lovely touch. The problem is feel. Every constellation is an island of 3 to 5 pegs, the wheel's interior is empty, and there is no brick or mover anywhere, so many shots touch a handful of pegs and drop. Fix: give the wheel itself some substance. Trace the painted inner ticked rim as 4 to 6 short brick arcs at the quadrants, or dot the dividers and the stick-figure lines with green-only or blue pegs, without touching the 35 candidates (ch-03). Acceptance: sweep mean at least about 7.5, and the 6-1→6-2 and 6-2→6-3 steps still resolve.

**6-3 The Seer's Chart (base-28), APPROVE with m2.** This is the stage's one game painting and it earns its place. The five-city loop and Mor Dhona's lines with glints are a clear, lore-right subject, and the chart detail (ships, kraken, compass roses) is lavish. Pacing is fine (sweep 8.5, bucket 92). The grade is the issue (m2). Reduce or drop the green on the sea areas so the land/sea read follows the source, or give the arch bricks a visible cloud crest. The "ring of moons round each city" is only partly there, because the ley lines break most rings, but the board still reads.

**6-4 Meteor Shower (base-29), APPROVE with m1.** This is the best pure spectacle in the stage. The streaks radiate correctly from the radiant knot, the Plough is traced, the snow range catches the light, and the 12 slide movers drift slowly along their own trains (36 units over 7 s), which matches the subject exactly. Movers arrive here for the first time, which is the right ramp beat before the finale. Fix the lake (m1). Sweep mean 7.0 is low but the movers make up for it; I'd not touch the layout.

**6-5 The Moon Gate (base-30), APPROVE on its own merits (M1, m3, n1 at stage level).** A strong finale. The ticked brick crown sits on the gate's rim, 16 orbit movers counter-rotate on two painted tracks, and the moon's phases (waxing up the left, waning down the right) are hidden under the pegs and revealed as the board clears, which is a real payoff (`cleared-1280`). Standing-stone lamp-posts and the stair path frame it well. Hard but fair: 17.4 per 48, greedy 12/48, finale gap 3.17, which matches MR 3's 3.31. Its only weakness is that 6-1 has already shown the same picture (M1). Fix that in 6-1, not here. Give the far moon some surface or glow (m3), and confirm in motion that the beams can be seen (n1).

## The stage as a set

- **Theme.** "The Silent Stars" holds: the instrument, the zodiac, the chart, the sky itself, then the gate. That is Urianger's reading of the heavens told in a sensible order. Every scene is set nowhere, and there is 1 game painting as the charter asks.
- **Variety.**
  - Mechanics ramp well: a static brick lattice, then pure pegs, then points and lines with arches, then slide movers, then orbit movers with a brick crown.
  - Looks: 6-2, 6-3 and 6-4 are distinct (navy/magenta, violet/green chart, navy/airglow). 6-1 and 6-5 are twins (M1).
  - Composition: three of five boards are a big centred ring or ellipse (6-1, 6-2, 6-5). That's thematic, but it puts more weight on M1's fix.
- **Ramp.**
  - Pooled scores are 24.7, 23.52, 22.6, 20.57 and 17.4, with steps of 1.18, 0.92, 2.03 and 3.17.
  - The mean is 21.76 against the charter's 21.6, within the band 25 → 17.
  - 6-2 → 6-3 resolves by only 0.01. If M2's fix makes 6-2 easier, it must not close that step.
- **Feel.** Stage 6 holds the two lowest sweep means in the game (6-2 at 6.4, 6-4 at 7.0). 6-4 is fine because its movers make it feel busy. 6-2 is not (M2).
- **Finale.** Earned and spectacular in motion. It needs only to stop sharing its image with the opener.

**What I didn't check:** motion in every level (the movers' feel, glints, beams and star masks) and real play feel. I judged from stills, the sweep figures and the play figures only.
