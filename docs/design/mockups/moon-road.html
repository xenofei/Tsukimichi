Tsukimichi: the Moon Road

Design v4 mockup by Mirei and Theo. Tsukimichi is the road of light the moon lays on night water. Everything you have walked is lit gold; everything ahead is dark water. Game art (icons, journal banners, zone art) is read from your own install at runtime. Here it is drawn as labelled stand-ins.

M PLUS 1p stands in for AXIS (body, captions) SAIRA EXTRA CONDENSED for TrumpGothic (eyebrows, rail, headers) Cinzel for Jupiter (titles) Rajdhani 118/160 for MiedingerMid (numerals) Square game icons are simplified stand-ins of the official ones (tribe emblems, expansion rings, quest-kind marks, job tiles).
